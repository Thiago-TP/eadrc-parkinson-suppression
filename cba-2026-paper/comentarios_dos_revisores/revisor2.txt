In this work, the authors apply ADRC to suppress involuntary wrist movements. The paper is interesting and well-written, with some minor suggestions to improve it presented in the sequel.

Authors should update the reference list.

The introduction is too long. Authors should focus on the disease's control aspects.

Please explain or provide a reference for the values of a1, a2, and a3 presented after (2).

How realistic is assuming the involuntary torque is purely sinusoidal? How will the presence of other harmonics impact the method?

Please define w after (11).

In the sentence before (22), the correct is "presented in Figure 2."

The discussion of the results is too brief. Reducing the number of methods in the comparison may provide the necessary space to enhance the discussion.
